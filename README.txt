REAL LIFE TRAGEDY — JAPAN OPEN WORLD (REBUILT)

This build is a functional rebuild of the uploaded prototype, not a text-only description.

Major changes:
- 36 x 36 playable city blocks.
- Japan-themed districts: Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Sapporo, Hokkaido Forest and Okinawa Coast.
- Named exploration landmarks shown on the full map and in-world marker beams.
- Enterable building blocks remain generated as points of interest, with dedicated mall, school and fire-station exteriors/interiors.
- Helicopter flight remains unrestricted by ground-building collision and supports altitude control.
- More traffic and pedestrian population, including phone users, tourists, vendors and joggers.
- Mission snapshots now save progress immediately at important stage transitions and periodically, covering courier, car theft, taxi, food, race, firefighter, boat race, treasure, marine survey and gang hunt jobs.
- Player position, current vehicle, owned vehicles, weapons/ammo, money, XP and active mission are persisted.
- Local WAV audio assets are included for pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle, car engine, helicopter rotor, tire skid and horn. They are local game audio files rather than oscillator-only beeps.

Run locally with a web server for best browser/PWA compatibility.
