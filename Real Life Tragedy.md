# Real Life Tragedy

Open-world crime sandbox. Steal cars, take jobs, outrun the law.

Move: WASD · E: enter car or shop · Space: punch / fire · Shift: run · J: jump · X: sit · G: draw gun · Drive: W/S gas+brake, A/D steer · Camera: drag, pinch or wheel, C = presets · Map: click minimap or M · Double-tap the job banner to skip a job

RLT · lightweight · installs as an app
